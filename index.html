Not perfect. Called.

The word incredible comes from the Latin incredibilis, “not believable.” It describes a man who lives differently: set apart, grounded, and unwavering.

Incredible Men,

I consider you an Incredible Man. Not because you’re perfect, but because you’re called. Called to lead with integrity and stand firm when life gets hard.

Whether we’ve met or not, I’m for you. I believe in you, and I’m praying that you step fully into who God created you to be.

You matter to God, and you matter to me.

6:33 Men · Saturdays · Lakeway

Seek first. Train hard. Stay together.

Most days, the noise gets first crack at you. On Saturday, we get up before the sun. We train hard side by side, pray together, and jump straight into the lake. It’s iron sharpening iron, before the world gets a say in your day.

It’s not a Bible study. It’s not a bootcamp. It’s both of those things and neither of those things.
